## What they do

Custom printing and decoration for local schools, sports teams, clubs, businesses, and community groups. Core work is done in-house at the Snohomish shop rather than outsourced.

**Services:**
- Custom apparel — screen printed tees, hoodies, hats, jackets, uniforms, team packs, event apparel
- Stickers and decals — die-cut stickers, labels, window decals, vehicle decals, safety labels
- Banners and signs — yard signs, banners, posters, wall graphics, event signage, sponsor boards, large-format
- Embroidery — polos, hats, jackets, bags, spirit wear, staff apparel
- Team stores — organized online ordering for schools, leagues, clubs, businesses, fundraisers
- Design and proofing — artwork review, cleanup, production-ready files, digital proofs

**Production methods:** screen print, DTF (direct-to-film), heat transfer, wide-format printing, embroidery, vinyl cutting.

**Fulfillment:** local pickup in Snohomish, shipping, or local delivery/team distribution.

## Team stores

A major part of the business. They run ~148 linked online stores for local organizations — mostly schools and youth sports teams in Snohomish County. Groups include Glacier Peak High School, Snohomish High School, and many youth leagues and clubs. Families find their team's store, order through it, and Streamline handles production and pickup logistics.

---

## Order flow

1. Customer uploads artwork or walks in
2. Shop reviews the art and sends a proof
3. Job goes into production once approved
4. Customer picks up, or it ships

For stickers specifically there's a faster "quick buy" path — upload art, pick size/finish/quantity, get a price estimate, submit.
